# Brand icons

SVG marks from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org), released under CC0 1.0, fetched
unmodified on 2026-11-17 from `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@<version>/icons/`:
`stripe.svg`, `revolut.svg`, `anthropic.svg`, `zendesk.svg` from 16.38.0; `n8n.svg` from
16.0.0, whose mark (connected nodes) is the one n8n is recognised by -- 16.38.0's renders
as an unrecognisable glyph at the diagram's size.

They are used by `scripts/make_landscape_svg.py` to name the services ResolveBilling
connects to. The marks remain their owners' trademarks; their use implies no partnership or
endorsement. Simple Icons has no mark for Zammad, Pennylane or OpenAI: the diagram writes
their names instead.
